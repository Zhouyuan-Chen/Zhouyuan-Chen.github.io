Sometimes, I really want to say that I hate everything I've been through, and everything that seems to work for everyone but me. Of course, I know it depends on many things: the effort and thought I put into my work, the way I communicate my ideas, timing and luck. I have to be better, I know, I have to work on that.

Sometimes, I really want to say that I hate all the things that waste my time and make life so difficult. But the thing is, I'll turn 30, and eventually 60, even if I get everything right and everything happens on time. I know things go wrong sometimes. Still, I've tried so hard to keep that from happening, and it's really frustrating when they go wrong anyway.

But sometimes, I just want to say, “FUCK OFF. I'm done.” Then, the next morning, I wake up and have to keep going as usual. I know it's unfair, but it is what it is.
